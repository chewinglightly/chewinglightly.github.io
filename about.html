Gem and Meg are artists and makers based in London, UK. Pals and, famously, palindromes of each other's names, they had been crafting away on their own jewellery before deciding to join forces. From there, Chewing Lightly was born.

While their styles may seem a little different on the surface, nature and whimsy are central points for both Gem and Meg's work. Using air dry clay, sterling silver, stainless steel and aluminium to create earrings, necklaces, bracelets and rings.

From the first sketches to the final pieces, everything is made with care, curiosity and experimentation. No two pieces are ever quite the same, and that's exactly how they like it. They love making jewellery that feels personal.

Chewing Lightly is jewellery to have fun with. Whether that's a little mushroom dangling from your ear or a big swirl hanging from your neck. Working closely, Gem and Meg bring their individual styles together to make characterful pieces designed to be worn, enjoyed and loved.
